Korean Beef Bowl


This delicious Korean beef bowl is quick and easy to make with ground beef. Season it with fresh ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sesame seeds, and crushed red pepper flakes, adjusting to your taste. Serve over warm rice or spiralized vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 5 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tablespoon freshly grated ginger
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • ½ cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup light brown sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 6 green onions, chopped, divided
  • 4 cups hot cooked brown rice
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add beef; cook, stirring and crumbling into small pieces until browned, 5 to 7 minutes. Drain excess grease.
  3. Stir in garlic, ginger, and sesame oil; cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in soy sauce, brown sugar, and pepper flakes; cook until beef absorbs some sauce, about 7 minutes.
  5. Add ½ chopped green onions.
  6. Serve over hot cooked rice; garnish with sesame seeds and remaining ½ green onions.
